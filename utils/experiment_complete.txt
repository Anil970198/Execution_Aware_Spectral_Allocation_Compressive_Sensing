PAPER THESIS (one sentence — every table/figure should point here)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In progressive Transformer CS reconstruction, parameter-efficient spectral
allocation is NOT necessarily execution-efficient; spectral capacity should be
allocated by reconstruction relevance relative to stage-dependent projection
burden. Controlled counterexample @ α=0.75:
  |σḡ|     → γ=1.066  (same quality)
  |σḡ|/(NE)→ γ=0.732  (Ours)
i.e.  α<1  ≠⇒  γ<1.

Method framing (locked):
  S_ℓj = |σ_ℓj ḡ_ℓj| / (N_ℓ E_ℓ)     ← execution-aware utility (priority)
  ΔP_ℓ = 4 E_ℓ                        ← parameter / structural budget (α pack)
  Realization (LOCKED, implementation-faithful):
    S_ℓj → 𝒮_ℓ (admitted indices) → shared-QKV init from 𝒮_ℓ → FT
  Claim / interpretation: principal effect = cross-block {r_ℓ}
    Matched top-r ablation (same r_ℓ, leading singular dirs) ≈ NE quality
  Do NOT claim that multi-CS main checkpoints were re-run as standard top-r
  unless those ckpts are actually regenerated that way.

Do NOT claim: NE uniquely keeps γ<1 (Uniform γ=0.75, |σ| γ=0.634 also <1).
DO claim: grad-aware WITHOUT execution norm can EXCEED dense QKV (γ>1); NE restores
sub-dense γ while preserving quality.

Do NOT put T2 legacy Uniform@α=0.50 (12-img Set11, 35.69) in main paper tables.
Latency: never claim universally faster; report @64 vs @256 honestly.

Takeaway (LOCKED):
  (1) λ-sweep: quality essentially flat; γ decreases monotonically as λ↑.
      λ=0.25 still yields γ>1 (1.015) — partial execution penalty is not enough.
      Full λ=1 (NE) restores γ=0.732. Supports using (NE)^1, not a milder power.
  (2) re (divide by E only): γ=1.109 ≈ classical 4E failure (T6 γ=1.111).
      Confirms: parameter-side / width-only denominators do NOT fix compute inflation.
  (3) rn (divide by N only): γ collapses to 0.440 but quality drops
      (Set11 −0.06 dB, Urban −0.10 dB vs NE). Over-penalizes late stages;
      N alone is too aggressive without the E factor that matches ΔC∝NE.
  (4) Positioning: strengthens that the *right* execution prior is ΔC∝N E
